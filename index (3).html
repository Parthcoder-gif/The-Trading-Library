Everything you may want to know before getting started.

Are these eBooks suitable for complete beginners?
Yes. The Trading Library is designed to take you from the fundamentals of trading to more advanced concepts. You can start with the first book even if you have little or no previous trading knowledge.
What exactly will I learn from the 4 eBooks?
You’ll learn the foundations of financial markets, charts and price action, technical and fundamental analysis, trading strategies, risk management, trading psychology, trade management, performance analysis and how to build a structured trading system.
In what order should I read the 4 eBooks?
For the best learning flow, read them in this order:
01 — THE MARKET AWAKENING → 02 — THE MARKET LANGUAGE → 03 — THE TRADING PLAYBOOK → 04 — THE PROFESSIONAL TRADER.
Each book builds on concepts introduced earlier.
Do the books cover technical analysis and price action?
Yes. THE MARKET LANGUAGE covers candlesticks, price action, trends, support and resistance, market structure, breakouts, technical indicators and technical analysis.
Is risk management covered in the Trading Library?
Yes. Risk management is covered throughout the series, including capital management, position sizing, stop-losses, risk/reward and more advanced risk-management concepts.
Do the books cover trading psychology and discipline?
Yes. The series covers mindset, discipline and the psychological side of trading, with THE TRADING PLAYBOOK and THE PROFESSIONAL TRADER going deeper into developing a structured and disciplined approach.
Can I read these eBooks on my phone or tablet?
Yes. The eBooks are designed to be easy to read digitally, so you can access them on compatible phones, tablets, laptops and computers.
